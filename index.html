What we teach

Every stage of school maths

Ages 7–11

KS2

Times tables, fractions, arithmetic fluency and SATs preparation — with a lot of attention paid to keeping maths feeling doable.

Ages 11–14

KS3

The bridge years, where gaps quietly open up. We find them early and close them before GCSE content starts stacking on top.

Ages 14–16

GCSE

Foundation and Higher tier, including resits, across AQA, Edexcel and OCR. Past-paper technique, targeted topic work, and the exam-day habits that protect marks.

Ages 16–18

A Level

Pure, Mechanics and Statistics, across AQA, Edexcel and OCR, taught by a current A Level teacher. Also useful for students needing maths support alongside Physics or Economics.
